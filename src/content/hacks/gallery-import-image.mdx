The home screen menu mixes app icons with photo tiles. Select a photo tile and it opens full screen. This hack adds your own photo as a new tile.

![Menu with a custom photo tile selected and labelled my photo](/images/hacks/menu-photo/menu-photo-tile.png)

## What you need

- Your badge and a USB-C data cable
- A photo on your computer
- An image editor: **Paint** on Windows or **Preview** on a Mac
- **VS Code** or another text editor

## How photo tiles work

Each tile uses two PNG files with the same name, plus one line of code:

| File on the BADGER drive | Size | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apps/menu/photos/my-photo.png` | 320 × 240 px | Shown full screen when you select the tile |
| `apps/menu/photos/tiles/my-photo.png` | 166 × 166 px (square tile) or 166 × 87 px (wide tile) | Shown in the menu grid |
| `apps/menu/gallery.py` | one line per photo | Tells the menu the tile's name, shape, and position |

The file name becomes the tile label, with dashes turned into spaces. `my-photo.png` shows as **my photo**. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only.

## 1. Make the two images

1. **Full-screen image (320 × 240)**
   - Crop your photo to a landscape 4:3 shape around the part you want to show.
     - **Windows (Paint):** choose **Select**, drag a box, then choose **Crop**.
     - **Mac (Preview):** drag a box, then choose **Tools → Crop**.
   - Resize it to exactly **320 × 240** pixels.
     - **Windows (Paint):** **Resize → Pixels**, clear **Maintain aspect ratio**, and enter 320 and 240.
     - **Mac (Preview):** **Tools → Adjust Size**, set the units to pixels, clear **Scale proportionally**, and enter 320 and 240.
   - Save it as a PNG named `my-photo.png`. In Preview, use **File → Export** and choose **PNG**.
2. **Tile image (166 × 166)**
   - Open your original photo again and crop a square around the subject.
   - Resize it to **166 × 166** pixels and save it as a PNG. Name this one `my-photo.png` as well, so keep it in a different folder from the first file.

The tile image doesn't have to match the full-screen image. Use a tight crop so the subject is easy to see in the small tile.

## 2. Copy the images to the badge

1. Connect the badge to your computer with a USB-C data cable.
2. **Enter USB Disk Mode.** Double-tap the badge's **RESET** button, or open **Mass Storage** from the menu. A drive named **BADGER** appears in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
3. Copy the 320 × 240 `my-photo.png` into `BADGER/apps/menu/photos/`.
4. Copy the 166 × 166 `my-photo.png` into `BADGER/apps/menu/photos/tiles/`.

## 3. Add the tile to the menu

1. Copy `BADGER/apps/menu/gallery.py` to your computer as a backup.
2. Open the **BADGER** drive in VS Code (**File → Open Folder...**) and open `apps/menu/gallery.py`.
3. Add a line for your photo at the top of the `GALLERY` list:

   ```python
   GALLERY = (
       ("my-photo", 2, 2, 2, None),
       ("mona-quilt", 2, 1, 7, (185, 100, 0.55)),
       ...
   )
   ```

   The values are:

   | Value | Meaning |
   | --- | --- |
   | `"my-photo"` | File name without `.png` |
   | `2, 2` | Width and height in grid cells. Use `2, 2` for a square tile (166 × 166 image) or `2, 1` for a wide tile (166 × 87 image) |
   | `2` | Position in the menu. `0` is the first tile, so `2` puts your photo near the top |
   | `None` | Use the centre of the photo when it opens. The built-in photos use `(x, y, zoom)` here to pick a focus point |

   Keep the comma at the end of the line.
4. Save the file and eject **BADGER**. The badge restarts. If it doesn't, press **RESET** once.

## Verify

- Move to your tile with the direction pad. The label at the bottom shows **my photo**.
- Press **Select** to open the photo full screen. Press any button to close it.

![Custom photo open full screen on the badge](/images/hacks/menu-photo/menu-photo-open.png)

## No-code alternative

To skip editing `gallery.py`, replace one of the built-in photos instead. For example, overwrite `photos/mona-centered.png` (320 × 240) and `photos/tiles/mona-centered.png` (166 × 166) with your own images. Keep the original file names. The label still reads **mona centered**. Keep copies of the originals if you want to put them back.

## Troubleshooting

- **The tile doesn't appear:** check that the tile image exists in `photos/tiles/` and that its name matches the `gallery.py` entry exactly, including lowercase letters.
- **The menu doesn't start after editing `gallery.py`:** there's probably a typo, such as a missing comma or quote. Double-tap **RESET** to get back to USB Disk Mode, then fix the line or restore your backup.
- **The photo looks stretched:** crop to 4:3 (full screen) or a square (tile) before resizing.
