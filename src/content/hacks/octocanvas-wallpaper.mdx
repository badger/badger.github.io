[Octocanvas](https://octocanvas.io) turns your GitHub profile into a wallpaper. It has a badge-sized download, so you can add it to the home screen menu as a wide photo tile that opens full screen.

![Menu with a wide Octocanvas tile selected](/images/hacks/octocanvas/menu-octocanvas-tile.png)

## What you need

- Your badge and a USB-C data cable
- A web browser
- An image editor: **Paint** on Windows or **Preview** on a Mac
- **VS Code** or another text editor

## 1. Generate the wallpaper

1. Open [octocanvas.io](https://octocanvas.io) and stay on the **Wallpaper** tab.
2. Enter your GitHub username and choose **Generate**.
3. Pick an **Avatar Filter** and a **Background Theme**.
4. Choose **Badge (320x240) Download PNG**. The file is saved to your Downloads folder as `github-wallpaper-<username>-small.png`.
5. Rename the file to `octocanvas.png`. It's already the right size for the full-screen view.

## 2. Make the wide tile image (166 × 87)

The menu tile is wider than it is tall, so crop the middle of the wallpaper around your avatar and name.

1. Open a copy of `octocanvas.png` in your image editor.
2. Select a wide box around your avatar and name, about 240 × 126 pixels, and crop to it.
   - **Windows (Paint):** choose **Select**, drag a box, then choose **Crop**.
   - **Mac (Preview):** drag a box, then choose **Tools → Crop**.
3. Resize the crop to exactly **166 × 87** pixels.
   - **Windows (Paint):** **Resize → Pixels**, clear **Maintain aspect ratio**, and enter 166 and 87.
   - **Mac (Preview):** **Tools → Adjust Size**, set the units to pixels, clear **Scale proportionally**, and enter 166 and 87.
4. Save it as a PNG, also named `octocanvas.png`, in a different folder from the full-size file.

## 3. Copy the images to the badge

1. Connect the badge to your computer with a USB-C data cable.
2. **Enter USB Disk Mode.** Double-tap the badge's **RESET** button, or open **Mass Storage** from the menu. A drive named **BADGER** appears in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
3. Copy the 320 × 240 `octocanvas.png` into `BADGER/apps/menu/photos/`.
4. Copy the 166 × 87 `octocanvas.png` into `BADGER/apps/menu/photos/tiles/`.

## 4. Add the tile to the menu

1. Copy `BADGER/apps/menu/gallery.py` to your computer as a backup.
2. Open the **BADGER** drive in VS Code (**File → Open Folder...**) and open `apps/menu/gallery.py`.
3. Add this line at the top of the `GALLERY` list:

   ```python
   GALLERY = (
       ("octocanvas", 2, 1, 3, None),
       ("mona-quilt", 2, 1, 7, (185, 100, 0.55)),
       ...
   )
   ```

   `"octocanvas"` is the file name without `.png`. `2, 1` makes a tile two cells wide and one cell tall. `3` sets its position in the menu, counting from `0`. `None` opens the wallpaper from its centre. Keep the comma at the end of the line.
4. Save the file and eject **BADGER**. The badge restarts. If it doesn't, press **RESET** once.

[Add Your Photo to the Menu](/hack/gallery-import-image/) explains each value and shows how to add square photo tiles.

## Verify

- Move to the wide tile with the direction pad. The label at the bottom reads **octocanvas**.
- Press **Select** to open the wallpaper full screen. Press any button to close it.

![Octocanvas wallpaper open full screen on the badge](/images/hacks/octocanvas/menu-octocanvas-open.png)

## Troubleshooting

- **The tile doesn't appear:** check that `photos/tiles/octocanvas.png` exists and the name in `gallery.py` matches it exactly.
- **The menu doesn't start after editing `gallery.py`:** check for a missing comma or quote. Double-tap **RESET** to get back to USB Disk Mode, then fix the line or restore your backup.
- **The tile looks squashed:** crop to a wide box (about 2:1) before resizing to 166 × 87.
