## Controls

- A and C: move through images
- B: show or hide thumbnails

## Add images

Place a 160×120 PNG in `images/` and a matching 60×45 PNG in `thumbnails/`.

## Files

```text
/apps/gallery/
├── __init__.py
├── icon.png
├── images/
└── thumbnails/
```
