## WebSockets Doom / Chocolate Doom engine

The WebAssembly engine and Emscripten loader are GPLv2 software. The reviewed
artifacts came from `danborden1/doom-web` revision
`e0ba81004e27e7d66141925dfebb091a558e2fe8`, which records the published
deployment and corresponding source revisions. The complete GPL text is in
`runtime/COPYING.txt`; corresponding source and deterministic adapter rebuild
details are in `runtime/SOURCE.md`.

The local adapter generated by `scripts/build-doom-runtime.mjs` supplies the
verified WAD and WebAssembly bytes directly, maps badge input to DOM keyboard
events, copies the engine framebuffer to the badge texture canvas, and exposes
lifecycle/save synchronization hooks.
