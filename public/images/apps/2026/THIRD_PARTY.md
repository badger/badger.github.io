# Pre-installed app icons

Generated from the badge firmware Menu icons (`apps/menu/src/icons.py`), which are derived from
[Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) (sharp, weight 500) by Google,
licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

`monas-expedition.svg` is a simple compass drawn for this site.
